CTP Car Accident Claims In Brisbane

Motor vehicle accidents on Brisbane roads — from the M1 and Gateway Motorway to suburban intersections — generate significant CTP claim volumes. AMK handles all CTP injury claims arising from Brisbane accidents.

The notice of claim must be lodged within 9 months of the accident. Early legal advice ensures the correct insurer is identified and the notice deadline is met. Our car accident lawyers page provides detailed information on the Queensland CTP scheme.

WorkCover And Workplace Injury Claims In Brisbane

Brisbane's diverse economy spans professional services, construction, healthcare, retail and logistics — all sectors with workplace injury exposure. Workers injured at work in Brisbane can access Queensland's workers compensation scheme.

WorkCover compensation covers weekly income replacement, medical and rehabilitation treatment, and — for serious injuries — lump sum compensation for permanent impairment and common law damages. Visit our WorkCover claims and workers compensation pages for further detail.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions.

What personal injury claims does AMK handle in Brisbane?

AMK handles CTP car accident claims, WorkCover and workers compensation claims, public liability claims, medical negligence claims, TPD and superannuation claims, and institutional abuse claims for Brisbane residents.

Is there a time limit for personal injury claims in Brisbane?

Time limits vary by claim type. CTP claims require a notice of claim within 9 months of the accident. WorkCover claims have early reporting requirements. Public liability and medical negligence claims are generally subject to a 3-year limitation period. Seek advice promptly.

Can AMK handle my Brisbane personal injury claim remotely?

Yes. Initial consultations and ongoing case management can be conducted by phone or video conference. We also attend Brisbane hospital and home visits for seriously injured clients.

What compensation is available for personal injuries in Brisbane?

Personal injury compensation can cover medical and rehabilitation expenses, past and future income loss, care and assistance costs, and general damages for pain, suffering and loss of amenity. The amount depends on injury type, severity and the specific claim scheme.

What does No Win No Fee mean for a Brisbane personal injury claim?

You pay no legal fees if your claim is unsuccessful. AMK advances all disbursements with no recovery if the claim fails. Queensland's 50/50 rule also limits legal costs to 50% of compensation recovered in most personal injury matters.
