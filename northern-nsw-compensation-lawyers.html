Your entitlements

NSW has strong protections
for injured people.

Workers compensation, motor accident, and personal injury claims in NSW are governed by distinct schemes with their own rules, deadlines, and entitlements. We know all of them.

Workers Compensation · icare · Workers Compensation Act 1987
Hurt at work?

In NSW, workers compensation is administered by icare. If you've been injured at work — whether on a construction site near Tweed Heads, in a warehouse in Lismore, or on a rural property — you have rights that icare must respect. Most workers don't know what they're fully entitled to.

  • ✓Weekly income support while you can't work
  • ✓All medical, hospital, and rehabilitation expenses
  • ✓Section 66 lump sum for permanent impairment
  • ✓Work Injury Damages (negligence claim) if WPI is 15% or more
  • ✓Dispute icare decisions at the Personal Injury Commission
Motor Accidents · SIRA · Motor Accidents Injuries Act 2017
Injured in a road accident?

NSW CTP insurance covers anyone injured on NSW roads — drivers, passengers, pedestrians, cyclists, and motorcyclists. The NSW scheme has two tiers: statutory benefits available regardless of fault, and common law damages for non-minor injuries.

  • ✓Statutory benefits: weekly income and medical expenses (regardless of fault)
  • ✓Common law damages for pain, suffering and economic loss
  • ✓Future care and help around the home
  • ✓Covers accidents on highways, rural roads, and local streets
  • ✓Advice on minor vs non-minor injury classification

Don't wait — time limits are strict

Miss a deadline and you
can lose your claim entirely.

NSW sets hard cut-off dates for every type of compensation claim. These aren't guidelines — they're legal deadlines. The sooner you call, the safer your position.

28
days
CTP — Register your accident

Register your motor accident with SIRA within 28 days to access immediate statutory benefits regardless of fault. Missing this window affects early treatment funding.

3
months
CTP — Lodge your claim form

Your formal motor accident claim form must be lodged within 3 months of the accident. Late lodgement requires explanation and can be refused. Don't delay.

6
months
icare workers compensation

Lodge your workers compensation claim with icare within 6 months of injury or diagnosis. Late claims can be rejected. icare must respond within 21 days of receipt.
