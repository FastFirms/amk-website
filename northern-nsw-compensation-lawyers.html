Road accidents we handle

Every kind of road accident
in Northern NSW.

NSW CTP insurance covers people injured on NSW roads, whoever they are and however they were travelling. If a motor vehicle was involved, you may have a claim under the Motor Accident Injuries Act 2017.

Car accidents

Drivers and passengers hurt in rear-end, intersection, head-on or single-vehicle crashes on highways, rural roads and town streets.

Motorcycle accidents

Riders and pillion passengers. Motorcycle injuries are often serious, and the insurer will look closely at fault. Get advice early.

Bicycle accidents

Cyclists hit by a car, a truck or an opening door, on the road or in a shared zone. CTP can cover you even though you don't hold a policy.

Pedestrian accidents

Struck by a vehicle on a crossing, a footpath, a driveway or in a car park. Pedestrians are covered by the NSW CTP scheme.

Passenger injuries

Hurt as a passenger, including in a family member's car. Claiming against their CTP insurer doesn't mean suing them personally.

Truck & heavy vehicle

Collisions with trucks, semi-trailers and heavy vehicles on the Pacific and Bruxner Highways and regional freight routes.

Bus, taxi & rideshare

Injured as a passenger or road user in an accident involving a bus, taxi or rideshare vehicle.

Hit-and-run

Injured by an unidentified or uninsured vehicle? You may still be able to claim through the Nominal Defendant.

Don't wait — time limits are strict

Miss a deadline and you
can lose your claim entirely.

The NSW CTP scheme sets hard cut-off dates. These aren’t guidelines — they’re legal deadlines. The sooner you call, the safer your position.

28
days
CTP — Register your accident

Register your motor accident with SIRA within 28 days to access immediate statutory benefits regardless of fault. Missing this window affects early treatment funding.

3
months
CTP — Lodge your claim form

Your formal motor accident claim form must be lodged within 3 months of the accident. Late lodgement requires explanation and can be refused. Don't delay.

3
years
CTP — Common law claim

A claim for common law damages generally must be made within 3 years of the accident. Late claims need a full and satisfactory explanation.
