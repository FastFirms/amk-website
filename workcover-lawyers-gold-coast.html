02

How The Queensland WorkCover Scheme Works For Gold Coast Workers

Queensland's WorkCover scheme is no-fault — injured workers are entitled to statutory benefits regardless of cause. Common law damages (for workers with at least 5% permanent impairment) require proof of employer negligence.

Report your injury to your employer immediately and lodge a WorkCover claim within 6 months of the injury or diagnosis. Benefits include weekly income replacement, medical and rehabilitation costs, and lump sum permanent impairment compensation. Time limits are strict.

03

What WorkCover Compensation Can Gold Coast Workers Receive?

WorkCover compensation for Gold Coast workers includes weekly income replacement, medical and rehabilitation treatment costs, permanent impairment lump sums, and — for eligible workers — common law damages.

Gold Coast hospitality and tourism workers may be vulnerable to income replacement caps under the statutory scheme if they work variable hours. Common law damages — available once the statutory process is exhausted for workers with at least 5% permanent impairment — can significantly increase total compensation for workers with ongoing disability.

04

Time Limits For Gold Coast WorkCover Claims

WorkCover claims should be lodged within 6 months of the injury. Late claims may be accepted where there is a reasonable excuse, but prompt action is strongly advised.

Early reporting also preserves workplace evidence — including incident reports, CCTV and witness accounts — that may be critical if the claim is disputed or escalates to common law proceedings.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions.

What is the time limit to lodge a WorkCover claim on the Gold Coast?

WorkCover claims should generally be lodged within 6 months of the injury or diagnosis. Report your injury to your employer immediately and seek legal advice promptly to ensure all deadlines are met.

Can Gold Coast workers claim common law damages as well as WorkCover benefits?

Yes. Workers with at least 5% permanent impairment who can establish employer negligence may pursue common law damages in addition to the statutory WorkCover benefits.

Does AMK handle disputed WorkCover claims on the Gold Coast?

Yes. AMK represents Gold Coast workers through disputed WorkCover claims, reviews and appeals — including disputes about liability, impairment ratings and treatment decisions.

Can theme park and resort workers make WorkCover claims on the Gold Coast?

Yes. All workers injured in the course of their employment — including tourism, hospitality, theme park and resort workers — are entitled to access the Queensland WorkCover scheme.

What if my Gold Coast WorkCover weekly payments are stopped?

WorkCover Queensland may reduce or cease weekly payments where it forms the view that a worker's incapacity has resolved. AMK can advise and assist with challenges to payment cessation decisions.
