Your entitlements

Two kinds of claim.
Both No Win, No Fee.

Injured workers and road accident victims in Ipswich have strong legal protections under Queensland law — most people don't realise how much they're entitled to.

Workers Compensation · WorkCover QLD
Hurt at work?

No-fault coverage applies from your first day. Whether it happened on a factory floor in Bundamba, a construction site in Springfield, or an office in the CBD — you have rights, and we'll protect them.

  • ✓Weekly wage replacement while you can't work 85% · 26 weeks
  • ✓All medical, hospital, and rehabilitation costs paid
  • ✓Common law lump-sum damages if employer negligence contributed needs 5% DPI
  • ✓Lost superannuation and future economic loss
  • ✓Pain, suffering, and loss of enjoyment of life
CTP · Motor Accident Insurance Act 1994
Injured in a road accident?

Every registered vehicle in Queensland carries CTP insurance that exists to protect you — even if you were partly at fault. Accidents on the Ipswich Motorway, Cunningham Highway, or any local street all qualify.

  • ✓Medical and rehabilitation expenses — all of them
  • ✓Past and future income you've lost because of your injury
  • ✓Pain, suffering, and loss of enjoyment of life
  • ✓Future care and help around the home
  • ✓Covers drivers, passengers, pedestrians, cyclists and riders

Don't wait — time limits are strict

Miss a deadline and you
can lose your claim entirely.

Queensland law sets hard cut-off dates for compensation claims. These aren't guidelines — they're legal deadlines. The sooner you call, the safer your position.

28
days
CTP — Early notification

Lodge an Accident Notification Form within 28 days of your road accident to access early treatment funding from the CTP insurer.

9
months
CTP — Formal claim

Your Notice of Accident Claim Form must be lodged within 9 months of the accident date, or within 1 month of first consulting a lawyer.

6
months
WorkCover claim

WorkCover Queensland claims should be lodged within 6 months of your injury or diagnosis. Late claims may be accepted — but don't rely on it.
