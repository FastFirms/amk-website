What we handle in Ipswich

Two types of claim.
Both on No Win, No Fee.

Whether you were hurt at work across Ipswich's industrial areas or in a road accident on the Ipswich Motorway or Warrego Highway, you likely have a claim — and a deadline.

Workers Compensation · WorkCover QLD
Injured at work?

Whether you work in manufacturing, logistics, construction, retail, or any other industry in Ipswich, a workplace injury entitles you to no-fault WorkCover benefits — and potentially a larger common law payout if your employer was negligent.

  • Weekly wage payments while you're off work 85% for 26 weeks
  • All reasonable medical and rehabilitation costs covered
  • Lump-sum common law damages if your employer was at fault requires 5% DPI
  • Pain and suffering, future economic loss, loss of superannuation
  • Covers: falls, machinery, repetitive strain, psychological injury, occupational disease
CTP · Motor Accident Insurance Act 1994
Injured in a road accident?

Every registered vehicle in Queensland carries CTP insurance that covers your injuries — even if you were partly at fault. Road accidents on the Ipswich Motorway, Cunningham Highway, or any local road can give rise to a claim.

  • Medical and rehabilitation expenses — fully covered
  • Past and future lost income, including superannuation
  • General damages for pain, suffering, and loss of enjoyment of life
  • Future care and domestic assistance costs
  • Covers: drivers, passengers, cyclists, pedestrians, riders
Act now — deadlines are strict

Miss a deadline in Queensland
and you can lose your claim entirely.

28
days
CTP Road Accident
Lodge an Accident Notification Form within 28 days to access early treatment funding from the CTP insurer.
9
months
CTP Claim Form
Notice of Accident Claim Form must be lodged within 9 months of your road accident date (or 1 month after first seeing a lawyer).
6
months
WorkCover Claim
WorkCover Queensland claims should be lodged within 6 months of injury or diagnosis. Late claims may be accepted but don't rely on it.
