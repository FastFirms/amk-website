What Types Of Compensation Claims Does AMK Handle In Toowoomba?

AMK Compensation Lawyers handles all major categories of personal injury compensation claim for Toowoomba and Darling Downs residents.

CTP (car accident) claims — injuries arising from motor vehicle accidents on the Warrego Highway, Toowoomba Range, New England Highway and all Darling Downs roads. WorkCover and workers compensation claims — workplace injuries across agriculture, transport, construction, mining support and healthcare. Public liability claims — injuries in shopping centres, public spaces, on footpaths and in commercial premises. TPD and superannuation claims — total and permanent disability claims for injured workers unable to return to work. Visit our workers compensation, WorkCover and public liability pages for detail on each claim type.

02

CTP Car Accident Compensation For Toowoomba Residents

The Toowoomba Range and Warrego Highway generate serious motor vehicle accidents involving passenger cars, trucks and heavy vehicles. CTP compensation is available for anyone injured through a driver's negligence on any Queensland road.

The most important deadline is lodging the notice of claim within 9 months of the accident. Our CTP lawyers ensure notice is lodged promptly and that all relevant evidence — including black box data in truck accidents — is preserved from the outset.

03

WorkCover And Workplace Injury Claims In Toowoomba

Agriculture, transport and logistics, construction and mining support are the dominant industries across the Darling Downs, and each generates significant workplace injury rates. Workers injured at work are entitled to access the Queensland workers compensation scheme administered by WorkCover Queensland.

WorkCover compensation covers weekly income replacement payments, medical and rehabilitation treatment, and — for workers with serious injuries — potential lump sum compensation for permanent impairment and common law damages. Time limits apply. Visit our WorkCover claims page for further detail.

04

Public Liability Claims In Toowoomba

Public liability law imposes a duty of care on businesses, local councils, shopping centres and property owners. If you are injured due to a hazard on someone else's premises or in a public space in Toowoomba, you may have a public liability claim.

Common public liability claims in Toowoomba include slips and falls in supermarkets and shopping centres, injuries caused by poorly maintained footpaths or parks, and accidents in commercial or recreational facilities. Visit our public liability lawyers page for further detail.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions.

What types of compensation claims does AMK handle in Toowoomba?

AMK handles CTP car accident claims, WorkCover and workers compensation claims, public liability claims, TPD and superannuation claims, and personal injury claims across all categories for Toowoomba and Darling Downs residents.

Is there a time limit for compensation claims in Toowoomba?

Time limits vary by claim type. CTP claims require a notice of claim within 9 months of the accident. WorkCover claims have their own reporting and claim lodgement deadlines. Public liability claims are generally subject to a 3-year limitation period. Seek legal advice promptly to avoid missing any deadline.

Does AMK handle truck accident compensation claims in Toowoomba?

Yes. Truck and heavy vehicle accident claims on the Toowoomba Range and Warrego Highway are a significant part of our CTP practice. These claims often involve complex liability and multiple insurer considerations.

Can I make a WorkCover claim for a Toowoomba workplace injury?

Yes. Workers injured in agriculture, transport, construction, healthcare and other industries across the Darling Downs can access the Queensland workers compensation scheme. AMK handles WorkCover claims on a No Win, No Fee basis.

Do I need to attend your office to start a Toowoomba compensation claim?

No. We can conduct your initial case assessment by phone or video conference. We also offer home or hospital visits for seriously injured clients across the Toowoomba region.
