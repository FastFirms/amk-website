About AMK Lawyers

Specialist Compensation Lawyers. On Your Side.

AMK Lawyers is a specialist personal injury and compensation law firm based in Ipswich, Queensland. We act exclusively for injured people — never for insurers, never for employers — on a 100% No Win, No Fee basis across Queensland and New South Wales.

Based in Ipswich, QLD Exclusively for injured people 100% No Win No Fee
01

Who We Are

AMK Lawyers was founded on a simple principle: injured people deserve quality legal representation regardless of their financial position. Operating from Augustine Heights in the Ipswich City Council area, we serve clients across Queensland — from Brisbane and the Gold Coast to Toowoomba, Ipswich and regional Queensland — as well as New South Wales. Every file is handled by experienced solicitors, not passed to juniors once you sign up.

02

What We Do

We handle the full spectrum of personal injury and compensation claims: motor vehicle accident claims, CTP claims, workers compensation and WorkCover claims, public liability claims, medical negligence, institutional abuse claims, TPD and superannuation claims, and broader personal injury claims. We also act for NSW clients in CTP and workers compensation matters.

03

Our Commitment to You

We take every case seriously — not just the high-value ones. Our No Win, No Fee guarantee is genuine: $0 upfront, all disbursements funded by us, and nothing payable if your claim is unsuccessful. We explain your options clearly, give you realistic advice, and never pressure you to accept an early offer that undervalues your claim.

04

Where We Serve

Our Queensland clients are primarily in Brisbane, the Gold Coast, Ipswich and Toowoomba, though we assist injured Queenslanders statewide. NSW clients can access our services through dedicated NSW service pages. Most consultations are conducted by phone or video; home and hospital visits are available on request.
