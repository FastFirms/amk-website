How Queensland CTP Insurance Works For Gold Coast Accidents

CTP insurance is mandatory for every registered Queensland vehicle. It provides compensation to people injured by the negligent driving of the insured vehicle. Your claim is made against the at-fault driver's CTP insurer.

The single most important deadline is lodging your notice of claim within 9 months of the accident. Note that accidents in the Tweed Heads area (NSW) may engage a different compensation scheme — if your accident crossed the Queensland-NSW border, seek specific advice on which scheme applies.

03

What Compensation Can Gold Coast Car Accident Victims Claim?

CTP compensation in Queensland covers medical and rehabilitation expenses, past and future loss of income, the cost of care and assistance, and general damages for pain, suffering and loss of amenity.

For Gold Coast residents in tourism, hospitality, construction and retail — sectors with significant physical demands — income loss claims can be substantial. The compensation available depends on injury severity, age, employment circumstances and the quality of medical and economic evidence assembled for your claim.

04

Time Limits For Gold Coast Car Accident Claims

You must give notice of your CTP claim within 9 months of the accident. The general limitation period for commencing proceedings is 3 years from the accident date.

CCTV footage from Gold Coast intersections and M1 cameras is typically overwritten within 30 days. Acting early preserves this evidence and gives your claim the strongest possible foundation. If your accident occurred close to the NSW border, time limits may differ — seek advice promptly.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions.

What is the time limit for a Gold Coast car accident claim?

You must give notice of your CTP claim within 9 months of the accident. The general limitation period is 3 years from the accident date. Act early to preserve CCTV and other time-sensitive evidence.

Can I claim if my Gold Coast accident was on the M1?

Yes. AMK handles CTP and motor vehicle injury claims arising from accidents on the M1 Pacific Motorway, Gold Coast Highway and all Gold Coast local roads.

What if my accident happened near the NSW-Queensland border?

Accidents in the Tweed Heads area may engage the NSW compensation scheme rather than the Queensland CTP scheme. Seek specific legal advice on which scheme applies to your circumstances.

Can I claim if I was partly at fault for the Gold Coast accident?

Contributory negligence reduces but does not necessarily eliminate your entitlement. Queensland law allows for apportionment of fault between parties.

Does AMK handle Gold Coast rideshare accident claims?

Yes. Claims arising from accidents in Uber, DiDi or other rideshare vehicles on the Gold Coast are handled on the same No Win, No Fee basis as other CTP matters.
