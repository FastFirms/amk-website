What WorkCover Claims Does AMK Handle In Brisbane?

AMK Compensation Lawyers handles the full range of WorkCover and workers compensation claims for Brisbane workers, including statutory benefit claims and common law damages claims.

Claim types include workplace injury claims for all injury categories — physical, psychological and industrial disease; statutory benefit claims for weekly income replacement and medical treatment during the WorkCover period; permanent impairment lump sum claims for workers with lasting disability; and common law damages claims for workers injured through employer or third-party negligence. Visit our WorkCover claims and workers compensation pages for detail on the Queensland scheme.

02

How The Queensland WorkCover Scheme Works For Brisbane Workers

Queensland's workers compensation scheme is a no-fault statutory scheme — injured workers are entitled to WorkCover benefits regardless of who caused the accident. Common law damages (available for workers with at least 5% permanent impairment) require proof of employer negligence.

The scheme is administered by WorkCover Queensland. Benefits include weekly statutory income replacement, payment of reasonable medical and rehabilitation costs, and lump sum compensation for permanent impairment. Time limits are strict — report your injury to your employer as soon as possible and lodge a WorkCover claim within 6 months of the injury or diagnosis.

03

What WorkCover Compensation Can Brisbane Workers Receive?

WorkCover compensation for Brisbane workers can include weekly income replacement payments, medical and rehabilitation treatment costs, permanent impairment lump sums, and — for eligible workers — common law damages for future economic loss and pain and suffering.

For Brisbane workers in professional roles, the income replacement cap under the statutory scheme may not fully compensate high-earning workers. Common law damages — available once the statutory process is exhausted for workers with at least 5% permanent impairment — can significantly increase total compensation for these workers.

04

Time Limits For WorkCover Claims In Brisbane

WorkCover claims should be lodged as early as possible. The statutory claim must generally be lodged within 6 months of the injury or date of diagnosis for occupational disease claims. Common law claims have additional time requirements.

Late claims may be accepted where there is a reasonable excuse, but the burden of demonstrating that excuse falls on the worker. Seeking legal advice promptly ensures all deadlines are identified and met. Evidence of workplace conditions and the circumstances of injury is also best gathered early.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions.

What is the time limit to lodge a WorkCover claim in Brisbane?

WorkCover claims should generally be lodged within 6 months of the injury or diagnosis. Late claims may be accepted where there is a reasonable excuse, but do not delay — seek advice promptly.

Can Brisbane workers claim common law damages as well as WorkCover benefits?

Yes. Workers with at least 5% permanent impairment who can establish employer negligence may pursue common law damages in addition to the statutory WorkCover benefits. Common law damages can significantly increase total compensation.

Does No Win No Fee apply to Brisbane WorkCover claims?

AMK acts on a No Win, No Fee basis for common law damages claims. Statutory WorkCover claim assistance is generally covered by scheme provisions. Contact us for a free assessment of your specific situation.

What if my Brisbane WorkCover claim is disputed?

WorkCover Queensland may dispute liability, the degree of impairment or the reasonableness of treatment. AMK handles disputed WorkCover claims and represents Brisbane workers through the review and appeal process.

Can I make a common law claim and a WorkCover claim for the same Brisbane workplace injury?

Yes, but the processes are sequential — statutory WorkCover benefits are accessed first, and common law damages are pursued once the statutory process is exhausted and permanent impairment is established.
