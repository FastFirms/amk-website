What we handle

We cover most types of
personal injury claim.

If you've been hurt through no fault of your own — or even partly your own fault — there's a good chance you have an entitlement. Here's what we specialise in.

Workers Compensation · WorkCover QLD & NSW
Injured at work

A workplace injury entitles you to no-fault statutory benefits — and potentially a common law lump sum if your employer was negligent. Covers offices, sites, warehouses, vehicles and beyond.

  • ✓Weekly wage payments while you recover
  • ✓All medical & rehabilitation costs covered
  • ✓Common law lump sum if employer was negligent
  • ✓Pain, suffering & future economic loss
Learn more →
CTP · Motor Accident Insurance Act
Road accident injury

Every registered vehicle carries CTP insurance that protects you — as a driver, passenger, pedestrian, or cyclist. You may have a claim even if you were partly at fault.

  • ✓Medical & rehabilitation expenses
  • ✓Past & future lost income
  • ✓Pain, suffering & loss of enjoyment of life
  • ✓Future care and domestic assistance
Learn more →
Public Liability · Occupier Liability
Slip, fall or public place

Property owners have a duty to keep premises safe. If you were injured in a shop, car park, or any public space, you may have a public liability claim against the occupier.

  • ✓Medical costs and out-of-pocket expenses
  • ✓Lost income during recovery
  • ✓Pain and suffering damages
  • ✓Future economic loss

Act now — deadlines are strict

Miss a deadline and you can
lose your right to claim entirely.

Queensland and NSW law sets hard cut-off dates. These are legal deadlines — not guidelines. The sooner you contact us, the safer your position.

28
days
QLD CTP — Early Notification

Lodge an Accident Notification Form within 28 days of a road accident to access early treatment funding.

9
months
QLD CTP — Claim Form

Notice of Accident Claim Form must be lodged within 9 months of the accident date.

6
months
QLD WorkCover

WorkCover Queensland claims should be lodged within 6 months of the date of injury or diagnosis.

3
years
Public Liability

Public liability personal injury claims in Queensland and NSW must be commenced within 3 years of the incident.

Common questions

Frequently asked about
the free claim check.

Honest answers to the questions most people ask before they call us.

What exactly is a free claim check? +
A free claim check is a no-obligation assessment of your situation. We ask a few questions about the incident, your injuries, and your circumstances, then give you an honest view of whether you have a legal claim and, if so, roughly what it may be worth. There's no cost, no pressure, and no commitment to proceed.
What does No Win, No Fee actually mean? +
It means exactly what it says. If we take on your claim and we don't win, you pay us nothing — no legal fees, no disbursements, no hidden charges. Our fee comes out of the settlement amount we negotiate for you. If there's no settlement, there's no bill. This removes all financial risk from making a claim.
What if I was partly at fault for the accident? +
Being partly at fault doesn't necessarily end your claim. In Queensland and NSW, compensation laws allow for contributory negligence — your award may be reduced by the proportion of your fault, but you can still receive meaningful compensation. Many successful claimants were partially responsible. The free claim check will tell you how this applies to your situation.
How long does a compensation claim take? +
It depends on the type and complexity of your claim. Straightforward WorkCover statutory claims can resolve in weeks. Common law and CTP matters typically take 12–36 months, depending on the insurer and injury severity. We'll give you a realistic timeline once we've assessed your claim.
I'm not sure if I have a claim — should I still contact you? +
Absolutely. "Not sure" is the most common reason people contact us, and that's exactly what the free check is designed for. We'll ask the right questions and give you a straight answer. Even if you don't have a claim, we'll tell you honestly rather than string you along. You have nothing to lose by asking.
What information do I need to get started? +
Very little. For the initial claim check we just need a general description of what happened, when, and the nature of your injuries. You don't need police reports, medical records, or any documents ready — we'll guide you on what to gather after we've confirmed a claim is viable. Just fill in the form or call us.
