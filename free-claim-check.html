What we handle

We cover most types of
personal injury claim.

If you've been hurt through no fault of your own — or even partly your own fault — there's a good chance you have an entitlement.

Workers Compensation · WorkCover
Injured at work

A workplace injury entitles you to no-fault statutory benefits and potentially a common law lump sum if your employer was negligent.

  • ✓Weekly wage payments while you recover
  • ✓All medical & rehabilitation costs
  • ✓Common law lump sum if employer was negligent
Learn more →
CTP · Motor Accident Insurance
Road accident injury

Every registered vehicle carries CTP insurance. You may have a claim even if you were partly at fault — as a driver, passenger, pedestrian or cyclist.

  • ✓Medical & rehabilitation expenses
  • ✓Past & future lost income
  • ✓Pain, suffering & loss of enjoyment
Learn more →
Public Liability · Occupier Liability
Slip, fall or public place

Property owners have a duty to keep premises safe. If you were injured in a shop, car park, or any public space, you may have a claim.

  • ✓Medical costs and out-of-pocket expenses
  • ✓Lost income during recovery
  • ✓Pain and suffering damages

Act now — deadlines are strict

Miss a deadline and you lose
your right to claim entirely.

Queensland and NSW law sets hard cut-off dates. Not guidelines — legal deadlines. The sooner you contact us, the safer your position.

28
days
QLD CTP — Early Notification

Lodge an Accident Notification Form within 28 days to access early treatment funding.

9
months
QLD CTP — Claim Form

Notice of Accident Claim Form must be lodged within 9 months of the accident date.

6
months
QLD WorkCover

WorkCover Queensland claims should be lodged within 6 months of the date of injury.

3
years
Public Liability

Public liability personal injury claims in Queensland and NSW must commence within 3 years.

Common questions

Frequently asked about
the free claim check.

Honest answers before you call us.

What does No Win, No Fee actually mean? +
If we take on your claim and we don't win, you pay us nothing — no legal fees, no disbursements, no hidden charges. Our fee comes out of the settlement amount. If there's no settlement, there's no bill. This removes all financial risk from making a claim.
What if I was partly at fault for the accident? +
Being partly at fault doesn't necessarily end your claim. In Queensland and NSW, compensation laws allow for contributory negligence — your award may be reduced by your proportion of fault, but you can still receive meaningful compensation. Many successful claimants were partially responsible.
How long does a compensation claim take? +
Straightforward WorkCover statutory claims can resolve in weeks. Common law and CTP matters typically take 12–36 months, depending on the insurer and injury severity. We'll give you a realistic timeline once we've assessed your claim.
I'm not sure if I have a claim — should I still contact you? +
Absolutely. "Not sure" is the most common reason people contact us — that's exactly what the claim check is designed for. We'll ask the right questions and give you a straight answer. Even if you don't have a claim, we'll tell you honestly. You have nothing to lose by asking.
What information do I need to get started? +
Very little. A general description of what happened, when, and the nature of your injuries is enough to start. You don't need police reports or medical records ready — we'll guide you on what to gather after we confirm a claim is viable.
