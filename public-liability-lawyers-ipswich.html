01

When Can You Claim For An Injury In An Ipswich Public Place?

If the person or business responsible for the place failed to take reasonable care, and that failure caused your injury, you may have a public liability claim.

Shopping centres, supermarkets, councils, landlords and businesses all owe a duty to keep their premises reasonably safe. Not every fall leads to a claim; the question is whether the hazard was something they should have found and dealt with.

For a broader explanation of these claims across the state, see our public liability page.

02

What Should You Do Straight After A Fall?

Report the incident, photograph the hazard and your injuries, collect the names of any witnesses, and get medical treatment the same day if you can.

Ask the centre or business to record the incident and keep any CCTV. That footage is frequently overwritten within days, so the request to preserve it is one of the first things AMK sends.

Keep the shoes you were wearing, your receipts and any notes about what you saw. Small details become important once the insurer starts asking questions.

03

How Long Do You Have To Bring A Claim?

Court proceedings must generally start within 3 years of the injury, but notice under the pre-court process is due much earlier.

Under the Personal Injuries Proceedings Act, the person responsible must be notified within 9 months of the incident, or within 1 month of first consulting a lawyer about it. Leaving it late makes the claim harder and the evidence weaker.

If you are not sure whether you are still in time, call us and we will tell you where you stand.

04

Are Council Footpath Claims Different?

They can be. Councils are not liable for every crack in a footpath, so these claims depend on what the council knew and what it did about it.

Evidence of earlier complaints, repair records and photographs of the hazard all matter. AMK investigates whether the defect had been reported and whether the council had a reasonable chance to fix it.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions.

I fell in an Ipswich shopping centre. Do I have a claim?

Possibly. It depends on whether the centre failed to take reasonable care, for example by leaving a spill unattended. Report the fall, take photos and ask for the CCTV to be kept.

How long do I have to make a public liability claim in Ipswich?

Court proceedings generally have to start within 3 years, but notice to the person responsible is due within 9 months of the incident or 1 month of consulting a lawyer. Get advice early.

Can I claim against Ipswich City Council for a footpath fall?

Sometimes. Councils are not responsible for every defect, so these claims depend on evidence about the hazard and what the council knew about it.

What if there were no witnesses to my fall?

You may still have a claim. Prompt reporting, medical records, photographs and CCTV can all support what happened.
