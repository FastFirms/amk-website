FAQ

Ipswich Claims — Common Questions.

Is AMK Compensation Lawyers based in Ipswich?

Yes. We operate from Augustine Heights within the Ipswich City Council area and have genuine local knowledge of Ipswich roads, employers, Ipswich Hospital and the Ipswich District Court.

What compensation claims can Ipswich residents make?

CTP claims from the Ipswich Motorway, Warrego Highway and Cunningham Highway; WorkCover and workplace injury claims across Ipswich's industrial and logistics sector; public liability injuries at Riverlink Shopping Centre or other venues; TPD cover inside superannuation; and general personal injury claims.

How quickly should I contact a lawyer after an Ipswich accident?

As soon as possible. CTP requires notice within 28 days. WorkCover must be lodged within 6 months. Hit-and-run Nominal Defendant claims require 3-month notice. Beyond those deadlines, claims are either extinguished or significantly harder to run.

Can I get a home visit if I can't travel?

Yes. We visit Ipswich clients at home or in Ipswich Hospital. We also conduct consultations by phone or video — free and with no obligation.

What does No Win, No Fee actually mean?

$0 upfront cost and nothing payable if your claim is unsuccessful. If we win your claim, our fees come from the compensation — not your pocket. We'll explain the full fee arrangement before you commit to anything.
