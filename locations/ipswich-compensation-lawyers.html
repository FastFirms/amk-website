Time Limits For Ipswich Compensation Claims

Compensation claims in Queensland are subject to strict statutory time limits. Missing a deadline can permanently extinguish a claim. Contact a lawyer as soon as possible after any accident or injury — even if you are not yet sure whether you have a claim.

Claim TypeKey DeadlineConsequence of Delay
CTP / motor vehicle Notice to insurer within 28 days of accident Late notice may be accepted but requires justification; delay weakens evidence
Hit-and-run (Nominal Defendant) Notice within 3 months Claims notified after 3 months may be statute-barred
WorkCover / workplace Lodge claim within 6 months of injury or diagnosis Late claims may be accepted with reasonable excuse; WorkCover may refuse late lodgement
Public liability Limitation period of 3 years from date of injury Claim extinguished after 3 years; evidence preserved better if action taken early
TPD (superannuation) Varies by policy; often no hard deadline but act promptly Policy conditions may impose time limits; insurer may argue deterioration post-eligibility

What Compensation Ipswich Claimants Can Receive

The compensation available depends on the type of claim. CTP and WorkCover claims operate under statutory schemes with defined benefit categories. Common law claims — available for more serious injuries — can deliver significantly higher compensation, including damages for future economic loss and pain and suffering.

Under the CTP statutory scheme, injured Ipswich motorists are entitled to income replacement (85% of normal weekly earnings up to a cap in the first 26 weeks, 75% thereafter), reasonable treatment and rehabilitation costs, and lump sum compensation for permanent impairment where the degree of impairment meets the statutory threshold. At-fault claimants receive reduced statutory benefits.

WorkCover statutory benefits include weekly income replacement, reasonable medical and rehabilitation costs, and a permanent impairment lump sum for workers with lasting disability. Workers who can establish employer negligence may also pursue common law damages (for injuries since 31 January 2015 there is no minimum impairment level), which can include past and future economic loss, general damages for pain and suffering, and medical expenses beyond the WorkCover period.

Public liability and TPD claim values vary significantly based on the degree of impairment, the financial impact of injury, and the terms of the relevant insurance policy. AMK provides a free assessment of likely compensation ranges at the initial consultation.

What are the time limits to make a claim in Ipswich?
