Brisbane Claim Deadlines You Need To Know

Queensland law sets firm deadlines for injury claims, and some start running from the day you are hurt. Speak with a lawyer early, even if you are unsure a claim is worth pursuing, so your options stay open.

Type Of ClaimTime LimitWhy It Matters
CTP / motor vehicle Notify the CTP insurer within 28 days of the crash A late notice must be explained, and delay can make evidence harder to obtain
Unidentified or uninsured vehicle (Nominal Defendant) Give notice within 3 months Notice outside this window can put the claim at risk
WorkCover / workplace Lodge your application within 6 months of the injury Late applications need a reasonable excuse and may be refused
Public liability Court proceedings generally within 3 years of the injury Once the limitation period passes, the right to sue is usually lost
TPD (superannuation) Depends on your policy — act early Policy terms and fund rules can affect eligibility over time

What A Brisbane Claim Can Cover

What you can recover depends on the scheme your claim falls under. Statutory schemes such as WorkCover pay set categories of benefits, while common law claims for negligence can include compensation for the full financial and personal impact of your injury.

For Brisbane workers, WorkCover can provide weekly compensation while you are off work, payment of reasonable medical and rehabilitation costs, and a lump sum offer if you are left with permanent impairment. If your employer's negligence caused the injury, you may be able to bring a common law claim on top of those benefits.

After a motor vehicle accident, a CTP claim against the at-fault driver's insurer can include past and future loss of earnings, medical and treatment expenses, care and assistance, and general damages for pain and suffering, subject to the limits set by Queensland legislation.

Public liability and TPD claims vary widely depending on the injury, its effect on your ability to work and the terms of any insurance policy. In your free assessment we explain which heads of compensation apply to you and what evidence will support them.

FAQ

Brisbane Compensation Questions, Answered.

Does AMK have an office in Brisbane?

No. AMK Compensation Lawyers is based at 15/20 Success Circuit, Augustine Heights, south-west of Brisbane. We act for Brisbane clients by phone and video, and we come to your home or hospital bed when getting around is difficult. You never need to travel to us to start a claim.

How long do I have to start a compensation claim in Brisbane?

It depends on the claim. After a car crash, the CTP insurer must be notified within 28 days. If the other driver cannot be identified, the Nominal Defendant must be told within 3 months. A WorkCover application should be lodged within 6 months of the injury, and most personal injury claims are subject to a 3-year limitation period. Getting advice early protects all of these.

Which claims does AMK handle for Brisbane clients?

Motor vehicle claims under Queensland's CTP scheme, WorkCover statutory and common law claims, public liability claims for falls and other accidents in shops, offices and public spaces, TPD claims through superannuation, and other personal injury matters.

What does No Win, No Fee mean at AMK?

You pay no legal fees unless your claim is successful. Nothing is charged upfront. When a claim succeeds, our fees come out of the settlement, and we explain the costs to you in writing before any work begins.

Can I deal with AMK entirely by phone or video?

Yes. Many Brisbane clients run their whole claim with us by phone, video and email. Where a face-to-face meeting helps, we can meet you at home or in hospital. Your first assessment is free.
