Car Accident & CTP Claims in Brisbane

Brisbane's road network — the M1 Pacific Motorway, Inner City Bypass, Gateway Motorway and Logan Motorway — sees thousands of collisions each year. If you were injured as a driver, passenger, cyclist or pedestrian, you may have a car accident compensation claim through Queensland's CTP scheme. AMK's CTP lawyers handle claims from initial notification through to settlement — including common law damages where your injury is serious. The 28-day notification deadline applies: contact us as soon as possible after your accident.

02

WorkCover & Workers Compensation Claims

Brisbane is home to construction, logistics, hospitality and healthcare workforces with above-average injury exposure. If you were hurt at work, WorkCover Queensland provides statutory benefits including weekly wages and medical expenses — and where your employer's negligence contributed, a common law damages claim may also be available. The 6-month statutory claim deadline makes early legal advice critical.

03

Public Liability Claims Across Brisbane

Slip and fall injuries at Brisbane's shopping centres, parks and public spaces — Queen Street Mall, Westfield Chermside, South Bank Parklands — are more common than insurers admit. If a business or council failed to maintain a safe environment, you may have a public liability claim. AMK investigates, gathers CCTV and incident records, and pursues the responsible party — at no cost to you unless we succeed.

04

Medical Negligence & TPD Claims

Brisbane's major hospitals — Royal Brisbane and Women's Hospital, Princess Alexandra Hospital, Mater Hospital — handle complex procedures where errors can cause serious harm. If substandard care contributed to your injury or condition, AMK's medical negligence lawyers can advise you. We also assist Brisbane residents with TPD superannuation claims when a permanent disability prevents a return to work.

FAQ

Brisbane Claims — Frequently Asked Questions.

Do I need to come into your office to start a claim?

No. Most Brisbane clients complete their initial consultation by phone or video call. We can also arrange a home or hospital visit if your injury prevents you from travelling.

How much does it cost to use AMK Lawyers?

Nothing upfront and nothing if your claim is unsuccessful. Every disbursement — medical reports, court fees, expert evidence — is funded by us and recovered only from your settlement.

Which Brisbane courts does AMK appear in?

We appear in the Brisbane Magistrates Court, Brisbane District Court and the Supreme Court of Queensland as required. The vast majority of matters settle before court, but we prepare every file accordingly.

How long do I have to make a compensation claim in Brisbane?

Time limits vary: 28 days to notify after a CTP accident, 6 months to lodge a WorkCover claim, and 3 years for most common law claims. Missing a deadline can extinguish your right — call us early.
