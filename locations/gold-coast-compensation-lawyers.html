Car Accident & CTP Claims on the Gold Coast

The Pacific Motorway (M1), Gold Coast Highway and the Southport–Burleigh corridor are high-traffic roads with a significant accident rate — particularly during peak tourist seasons. If you were injured as a driver, passenger, cyclist or pedestrian, AMK's car accident lawyers can advise you on your CTP entitlements. Our CTP claim specialists handle both statutory and common law claims across the Gold Coast. Don't miss the 28-day notification window.

02

WorkCover & Workers Compensation on the Gold Coast

The Gold Coast's hospitality, construction and tourism sectors carry a high workplace injury burden. If you were hurt on the job — at a hotel, theme park, construction site or warehouse — WorkCover Queensland covers your medical expenses and lost wages. Where negligence was a factor, a common law damages claim may substantially increase what you recover. Lodge within 6 months of injury.

03

Public Liability at Gold Coast Venues & Attractions

Slips and falls at Pacific Fair, Robina Town Centre, Surfers Paradise and along the Gold Coast beachfront are not uncommon. Businesses and local councils have a duty to maintain safe premises. If their failure injured you, AMK's public liability lawyers can investigate, collect evidence before it disappears, and run your claim from start to settlement.

04

Medical Negligence & TPD Claims

Gold Coast University Hospital (GCUH) and the network of day surgeries and specialist clinics across Southport and Robina handle a high volume of procedures. Where substandard care caused your injury, AMK's medical negligence team can advise on your prospects. We also assist Gold Coast residents with TPD and superannuation claims where a permanent disability has ended their working life.

FAQ

Gold Coast Claims — Frequently Asked Questions.

Can AMK Lawyers help Gold Coast residents?

Yes. We act for Gold Coast clients in CTP, WorkCover, public liability, medical negligence and TPD claims. Most consultations are by phone or video so you don't need to travel.

Is there a Gold Coast time limit for compensation claims?

Yes. CTP claims require a 28-day notification. WorkCover claims must be lodged within 6 months. Common law claims generally have a 3-year limit. Early advice is critical.

What if I was injured at a Gold Coast resort or tourist attraction?

If a business or operator failed to maintain a safe environment, you likely have a public liability claim regardless of whether you are a resident or visitor. We assess this for free.

How does No Win No Fee work?

You pay nothing upfront. We fund all disbursements and recover them only from your settlement. If your claim is unsuccessful, you owe us nothing.
