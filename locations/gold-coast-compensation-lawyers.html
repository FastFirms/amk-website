01

How Injuries Happen On The Gold Coast

Most Gold Coast compensation matters we see start in one of three places: on the road, at work, or on someone else's premises. Each one is governed by a different Queensland scheme with its own rules, insurer and deadlines.

The Pacific Motorway and the Gold Coast Highway carry heavy commuter, freight and visitor traffic, and the city's growth means long stretches of roadworks and congested interchanges. When a crash is caused by another driver, the compulsory third party insurer of that vehicle responds — and it is that insurer, not the driver, we deal with on your behalf. Our car accident page explains the steps in more depth.

The local economy leans on hospitality, tourism, retail and construction. Those industries involve manual handling, long shifts, hot kitchens, wet floors and work at heights, so lifting injuries, burns, falls and repetitive strain are common. Every Queensland employer must carry WorkCover insurance, which means an injured worker usually has a claim even when nobody was at fault.

Shopping centres, hotels, attractions and foreshore areas host large crowds every day. When a hazard is left unattended — a spill, a broken step, poor lighting — and someone is hurt, the occupier and its insurer may be liable. Evidence in these cases fades fast, so early contact matters.

Deadlines That Apply To Gold Coast Claims

Queensland law sets firm windows for notifying insurers and starting proceedings. Some are measured in weeks, not years. If you are unsure whether a deadline has passed, call before assuming it is too late.

Type Of ClaimTime LimitWhy It Matters
Motor vehicle (CTP) Notify the CTP insurer within 28 days of the crash A late notice needs a reasonable explanation, and witnesses and footage become harder to find
Unidentified or uninsured vehicle Notify the Nominal Defendant within 3 months Missing this window can bar the claim entirely
Work injury (WorkCover) Apply within 6 months of the injury or diagnosis Applications outside this period can be rejected unless there is a good reason for the delay
Injury in a public place Generally 3 years from the date of injury After the limitation period, the right to sue is usually lost
TPD through super Set by each policy — act promptly Policy terms and fund rules can impose conditions that are easy to miss

What A Gold Coast Claim Can Cover

Compensation is designed to restore what the injury has taken from you — income, health costs and quality of life. The heads of damage available depend on which scheme applies and how serious the injury is.

In a road accident claim, an injured person can usually recover lost wages and lost future earning capacity, medical, physiotherapy and rehabilitation costs, travel to treatment, the value of care and help from family, and an amount for pain, suffering and loss of enjoyment of life.

A WorkCover claim pays weekly benefits while you are off work and covers approved treatment. If the injury leaves a permanent impairment, a lump sum may follow. Where an employer's negligence contributed, a separate common law claim can add damages for future economic loss and general damages.

Every claim is valued on its own facts. In your free assessment we explain which categories apply to you and what evidence will be needed to prove them.

FAQ

Gold Coast Claims — Your Questions Answered.

Does AMK have a Gold Coast office?

No. Our office is at 15/20 Success Circuit, Augustine Heights. We act for Gold Coast clients by phone, email and video, and we visit you at home or in hospital when your injury makes that the better option.

How long do I have to make a claim after a Gold Coast accident?

For a car accident, the CTP insurer must be notified within 28 days. If the vehicle cannot be identified or was uninsured, notice to the Nominal Defendant is due within 3 months. Work injury claims should be lodged with WorkCover within 6 months. Most public liability claims must be started within 3 years. Speak to a lawyer early so none of these windows close on you.

I was injured at work on the Gold Coast but my employer says it was my fault. Can I still claim?

Usually, yes. WorkCover Queensland is a no-fault scheme, so statutory benefits do not depend on who caused the injury. Fault becomes relevant if you later pursue common law damages against your employer, which we can assess for you.

Will I have to pay anything upfront?

No. Our compensation work is done on a 100% No Win, No Fee basis. You pay no professional fees unless the claim succeeds, and any costs are deducted from your settlement under a written costs agreement you approve first.

Can you see me if I am in Gold Coast University Hospital?

Yes. We can arrange a hospital or home visit when you are not able to travel, or speak with you by phone or video if that suits you better. The first assessment is free.

What if the insurer has already denied my claim?

A denial is not always the end. Insurers can be wrong about liability, the extent of an injury or what treatment is reasonable. We review the decision, explain your options for review or challenge, and act on them if the case has merit.
