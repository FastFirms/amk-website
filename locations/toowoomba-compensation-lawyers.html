Car Accident & CTP Claims in Toowoomba

The Warrego Highway, Gore Highway and Toowoomba Range access roads have a history of serious accidents — particularly heavy vehicle collisions in adverse weather. If you were injured in a car accident in or around Toowoomba, Queensland's CTP scheme covers you regardless of fault. AMK's car accident lawyers handle the full claim process, including serious injury common law damages. Notify within 28 days of your accident.

WorkCover & Agricultural Workplace Claims

Toowoomba's agricultural, logistics and mining sectors carry a disproportionate workplace injury burden. Farm workers, transport drivers and construction workers on the Darling Downs have the same WorkCover Queensland entitlements as any other worker — and where employer negligence contributed, a common law damages claim may deliver substantially more. Lodge your WorkCover claim within 6 months.

Public Liability & Personal Injury Claims

Slip and fall injuries at Grand Central, Clifford Gardens or Toowoomba's many public spaces may give rise to a public liability claim against the premises operator or Toowoomba Regional Council. FAQ

Toowoomba Claims — Frequently Asked Questions.

Can AMK Lawyers help Toowoomba residents?

Yes. We act for Toowoomba and Darling Downs residents remotely — by phone or video — or by home visit where needed. All claims are handled on a No Win, No Fee basis.

Is the Warrego Highway covered by Queensland CTP?

Yes. All registered Queensland vehicles are covered by CTP insurance on any road. If you were injured on the Warrego Highway or Gore Highway near Toowoomba, the CTP scheme applies.

What are the claim time limits for Toowoomba residents?

28 days to notify after a CTP accident; 6 months to lodge a WorkCover claim; 3 years for most common law personal injury claims. Agricultural and construction workers should note that the 6-month WorkCover window runs from the date of injury.

Can AMK help with farm and agricultural workplace injuries?

Yes. Agricultural and farm workers on the Darling Downs are covered by WorkCover Queensland and have the same compensation rights as any other Queensland worker.
