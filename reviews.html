Why Client Reviews Matter in Compensation Law

Choosing a compensation lawyer is one of the most important decisions you'll make after an injury. The wrong firm — one that prioritises volume over outcomes, or pushes early settlements to close files quickly — can cost you tens or hundreds of thousands of dollars. Client reviews are an unfiltered signal of how a firm actually operates.
