01

What No Win No Fee Means For Toowoomba Claimants

A No Win No Fee arrangement means you pay nothing unless your claim succeeds. AMK advances all disbursements — medical reports, expert evidence, court filing fees — with no interest charged and no recovery if your claim fails.

Queensland's 50/50 rule under the Personal Injuries Proceedings Act 2002 provides a statutory protection: your legal costs cannot exceed 50% of the compensation recovered in most personal injury matters. This applies to all Toowoomba and Darling Downs claims regardless of any fee arrangement.

02

Toowoomba Claims Eligible For No Win No Fee

AMK offers No Win No Fee arrangements for all major personal injury claim types across Toowoomba and the Darling Downs.

CTP car accident claims — accidents on the Warrego Highway, Toowoomba Range, New England Highway and all local roads. WorkCover and workers compensation — workplace injuries in agriculture, transport, construction, healthcare and all Darling Downs industries. Public liability claims — slips, falls and other injuries in shopping centres, public spaces and commercial premises. Visit our No Win, No Fee page for further information on how the arrangement works.

03

How AMK Protects Toowoomba Clients Under No Win No Fee

AMK's No Win No Fee guarantee covers both professional fees and disbursements. You will not be invoiced for any costs while your claim is running, and no recovery is made against you if your claim is unsuccessful.

The 50/50 rule provides an additional floor of protection: in most personal injury matters, the total legal costs (professional fees plus disbursements) cannot exceed half of your compensation. This ensures your net compensation is protected even in complex, high-cost matters.

04

Compensation Available To Toowoomba Claimants

Personal injury compensation can cover medical and rehabilitation expenses, past and future income loss, care and assistance costs, and general damages for pain, suffering and loss of amenity.

For Toowoomba workers in agriculture, transport, construction and mining support, economic loss claims for serious injuries — including lost wages and reduced earning capacity — can be substantial. The compensation available depends on claim type, injury severity and the quality of evidence assembled.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions.

What does No Win No Fee mean for a Toowoomba compensation claim?

No Win No Fee means you pay no legal fees if your claim is unsuccessful. AMK also advances disbursements — expert reports, medical records, court costs — with no recovery against you if the claim fails.

Does the 50/50 rule apply to Toowoomba claims?

Yes. Queensland's 50/50 rule means total legal costs cannot exceed 50% of the compensation recovered in most personal injury matters, including all Toowoomba and Darling Downs claims.

What types of Toowoomba claims are covered by No Win No Fee?

CTP car accident claims, WorkCover and workers compensation claims, public liability claims and personal injury claims — all claim types handled by AMK in Toowoomba are taken on a No Win, No Fee basis.

Can I start a Toowoomba claim without coming to your office?

Yes. We can complete your free case assessment by phone or video. We also offer home or hospital visits for seriously injured Toowoomba and Darling Downs clients.

How quickly should I contact AMK after a Toowoomba injury?

As soon as possible. CTP claims require notice within 9 months of the accident, and WorkCover claims have early reporting deadlines. Evidence — including CCTV and witness accounts — can also be lost quickly after an incident.
