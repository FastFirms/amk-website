02

CTP And Motor Vehicle Injury Claims On The Gold Coast

The Gold Coast road network generates significant CTP claim volumes, particularly on the M1 Pacific Motorway, Gold Coast Highway and entertainment district roads. AMK handles all CTP and motor vehicle injury claims arising from Gold Coast accidents.

The notice of claim must be lodged within 9 months of the accident. For accidents near the NSW border, the applicable compensation scheme may differ — seek specific legal advice promptly. Visit our car accident lawyers page for detail on the Queensland CTP scheme.

03

WorkCover And Workplace Injuries On The Gold Coast

Tourism, hospitality, construction, retail and healthcare are the dominant Gold Coast employment sectors, each with distinct workplace injury profiles. Workers injured at work on the Gold Coast can access Queensland's workers compensation scheme.

WorkCover compensation covers weekly income replacement, medical and rehabilitation treatment, and — for serious injuries — lump sum compensation and common law damages. Theme park, resort and hospitality workers have particular exposures worth specific advice. Visit our WorkCover claims page for further detail.

04

Public Liability Claims On The Gold Coast

Gold Coast businesses — particularly in tourism, entertainment and retail — owe a duty of care to visitors and customers. Injuries in theme parks, shopping centres, restaurants, resorts and on public footpaths may give rise to public liability claims.

Common Gold Coast public liability incidents include slips in wet-floor areas in theme parks and resorts, falls caused by poorly maintained footpaths, and injuries in entertainment venues. Visit our public liability lawyers page for further information.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions.

What personal injury claims does AMK handle on the Gold Coast?

AMK handles CTP car accident claims, WorkCover and workers compensation claims, public liability claims, medical negligence claims, and TPD and superannuation claims for Gold Coast residents and visitors injured on the Gold Coast.

Is there a time limit for personal injury claims on the Gold Coast?

Time limits vary by claim type. CTP claims require notice within 9 months of the accident. WorkCover has early reporting deadlines. Public liability and medical negligence claims are generally subject to a 3-year limitation period.

Can visitors to the Gold Coast make personal injury claims?

Yes. Tourists and visitors injured in theme parks, resorts, shopping centres or on Gold Coast roads may have claims — whether CTP, public liability or WorkCover depending on the circumstances.

What compensation is available for Gold Coast personal injuries?

Compensation can cover medical and rehabilitation expenses, income loss, care and assistance costs, and general damages for pain and suffering. The amount depends on injury severity and the specific claim type.

Does AMK handle theme park and resort injury claims on the Gold Coast?

Yes. Public liability claims arising from injuries in theme parks, resorts, shopping centres and entertainment venues are handled on a No Win, No Fee basis.
