01

Workers Compensation Entitlements For Gold Coast Employees

Queensland's workers compensation scheme entitles injured Gold Coast workers to statutory benefits regardless of fault — weekly income replacement, medical and rehabilitation costs, and lump sum permanent impairment compensation.

Workers who can establish employer negligence may also pursue common law damages. The Gold Coast's tourism, hospitality and construction sectors present particular injury profiles, including manual handling injuries, falls from height, heat-related illness and injuries in kitchen and food preparation environments. Visit our workers compensation page for detail.

02

Common Law Damages For Gold Coast Workers

Common law damages are available to Gold Coast workers who can establish employer negligence. For injuries since 31 January 2015 there is no minimum impairment level. Common law compensation can significantly exceed the statutory scheme benefits.

Common law claims can cover future economic loss — including reduced earning capacity for Gold Coast workers in trade, hospitality and construction whose injuries affect their physical capacity — as well as medical expenses, care costs and general damages for pain and suffering. AMK prepares and negotiates common law claims for Gold Coast workers across all industry sectors.

03

Workplace Injury Types On The Gold Coast

Gold Coast's employment sectors produce distinctive workplace injury patterns — particularly in hospitality, tourism, construction and healthcare.

Common Gold Coast workplace injuries include slip and fall injuries for hospitality and hotel workers, musculoskeletal injuries from manual handling in construction and warehousing, heat stress and dehydration injuries in outdoor and kitchen environments, psychological injuries in tourism and service industry roles, and injuries from equipment failure or unsafe premises in commercial kitchens and resort facilities.

04

Time Limits For Gold Coast Workers Compensation Claims

Report your injury to your employer as soon as possible and lodge your WorkCover claim within 6 months of the injury or diagnosis.

Late claims may be accepted where there is a reasonable excuse, but prompt action is strongly advised. Early reporting also preserves workplace evidence — including incident reports, CCTV and witness accounts — that may be critical in a disputed claim or common law proceedings.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions.

What workers compensation benefits are available to Gold Coast workers?

Gold Coast workers are entitled to weekly income replacement, medical and rehabilitation costs, and lump sum permanent impairment compensation. Workers who can prove employer negligence may also pursue common law damages.

Can Gold Coast hospitality and tourism workers make WorkCover claims?

Yes. All workers injured in the course of employment — including hospitality, hotel, theme park and resort workers — are entitled to WorkCover statutory benefits regardless of the cause of the accident.

What is the time limit for a Gold Coast workers compensation claim?

Lodge your WorkCover claim within 6 months of the injury or diagnosis, and report the injury to your employer immediately. Common law claims have additional time requirements — seek advice promptly.

Can Gold Coast workers claim for psychological injuries?

Yes. Psychological injuries arising from workplace bullying, harassment or traumatic events are compensable under the Queensland workers compensation scheme, subject to specific eligibility requirements.

Does AMK handle self-insured employer claims on the Gold Coast?

Yes. AMK handles workers compensation claims against self-insured Gold Coast employers as well as WorkCover Queensland-managed claims.
