What Does No Win No Fee Mean For Gold Coast Residents?

A No Win No Fee arrangement means you pay your lawyers nothing unless your compensation claim succeeds. If your claim does not result in compensation, you owe no legal fees.

AMK Compensation Lawyers operates on a genuine No Win, No Fee basis for all personal injury claims on the Gold Coast. We advance all disbursements — medical reports, expert evidence, court filing fees — throughout the life of your claim. If your claim is unsuccessful, those costs are not passed back to you. Queensland's 50/50 rule also provides a statutory protection ensuring that your legal costs cannot exceed 50% of your compensation in most personal injury matters.

AMK Compensation Lawyers offers No Win No Fee arrangements for the full range of Gold Coast personal injury compensation claims.

WorkCover and workers compensation claims — workplace injuries in tourism, hospitality, construction and all Gold Coast industries. Public liability claims — slips, falls and other injuries in Gold Coast shopping centres, theme parks, resorts and public spaces. Personal injury claims — including medical negligence and institutional abuse. Visit our No Win, No Fee page for further information.

03

How AMK's No Win No Fee Arrangement Protects Gold Coast Clients

Unlike some fee arrangements, AMK's No Win No Fee guarantee covers disbursements as well as professional fees. You are not billed for expert reports, medical records or court costs while your matter is running.

The 50/50 rule under Queensland's Personal Injuries Proceedings Act 2002 provides an additional statutory protection: your legal costs cannot exceed 50% of the compensation recovered in most personal injury matters. This rule applies regardless of any fee agreement, providing a floor of protection for injured Gold Coast residents.

04

What Compensation Can Gold Coast Claimants Receive?

Personal injury compensation in Queensland can cover medical and rehabilitation expenses, past and future income loss, the cost of care and assistance, and general damages for pain, suffering and loss of amenity.

The compensation available depends on the type of claim, the severity of injury, the claimant's age and employment circumstances, and the quality of the evidence assembled. For Gold Coast workers in tourism, hospitality, construction and marine industries, economic loss claims can be substantial for serious injuries.
