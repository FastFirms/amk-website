FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions.

What does No Win, No Fee mean?

If your case isn't successful, you won't pay our legal fees. We only get paid when you do. No hidden costs, no surprises.

How much does the first assessment cost?

Nothing. Tell us what happened and we'll assess your situation for free and tell you exactly where you stand, with no obligation.

What types of compensation claims does AMK handle?

Motor vehicle accidents and CTP claims, workplace accidents and WorkCover claims, public liability claims, medical negligence, institutional abuse, and TPD and superannuation claims.

Where are AMK's offices?

Augustine Heights (15/20 Success Circuit), Surfers Paradise (Level 13, 50 Cavill Avenue) and South Toowoomba (58–62 Water Street). We also act by phone, video and home or hospital visits across Queensland.

How do I start a claim?

Use the free claim check on this page or call 07 3136 3287. We'll assess your situation, then gather evidence, manage paperwork and deal with insurers on your behalf.
